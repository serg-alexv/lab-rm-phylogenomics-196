## Family-specific multipart TypeII amendment

The original exact-one-gene IIG condition was too broad as a statement about biology. [Furuta et al.2011, Results and Fig5](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0018819) explicitly describes comparative-genomic TypeIIG systems with a separate specificity subunit. That evidence establishes a predicted family architecture, rather than experimental activity of a panel locus. [The BsaXI primary study](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/microbiology/articles/10.3389/fmicb.2022.888435/full) instead classifies BsaXI as TypeIIB and reconstitutes activity from separate fused-RM and S subunits. BcgI/BsaXI-like fusion must therefore not automatically be called IIG. [The original nomenclature](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkg274) allows overlapping TypeII subtypes; fused restriction/methylation alone does not establish the IIG biochemical designation.

The frozen candidate scope and profile manifest contain no exact BcgI/BsaXI-labelled assignment. PADLOC `RM_type_IIG` has a single `REase_MTase_IIG` core and prohibits `Specificity_I`; its TypeII model instead has optional `Specificity_II_00001` through `00006`. The exact DF IIG definitions list numbered/FAM profiles without a separate-S component. None of these model facts proves that an actual numbered profile belongs to the separate-S family in Furuta et al. Native labels remain proposals; the ordinary single-chain reviewed native path remains available unchanged.

For an actually supported multipart TypeII family, a native candidate can be expanded using the existing executed neighborhood-review fields, or an explicit manual candidate can reuse full-scope native domains. Complete classification additionally requires `candidate_review.type_ii_family_architecture`: schema `RM_MULTIPART_TYPE_II_FAMILY_ARCHITECTURE_V1`, architecture `FUSED_RM_SEPARATE_SPECIFICITY`, a bounded `family_id`, `reviewed_subtype` (`IIB`, `IIG`, or `UNRESOLVED_TYPE_II_SUBTYPE`), exact different `rm_locus_key` and `specificity_locus_key`, `primary_family_basis_refs`, and `independent_family_review_ref`. The exact candidate comprises those two source loci; REASE and MTASE roles must both bind the RM locus and SPECIFICITY must bind the separate S locus. Required fusion enum is `REVIEWED_SUPPORTED_TYPE_II_FUSION`; recognition, compatibility, intact domains, source flags, native thresholds, coordinates, full-scope accounting and overlap review remain enforced. A generic nearby S gene, or unsupported fusion, cannot satisfy the family path.

The opened independent witness has schema `RM_MULTIPART_TYPE_II_FAMILY_REVIEW_V1`, execution `EXECUTED_INDEPENDENT_SOURCE_DOMAIN_FAMILY_REVIEW`, decision `SUPPORTED_FUSED_RM_SEPARATE_SPECIFICITY`, `independent_of_curation_producer=true` and a nonempty independent reviewer different from `curation_reviewer_id`. It binds assembly/candidate/family/reviewed-subtype, RM/S locus and AA hashes, all three roles' exact `evidence_hit_ids`, `native_model_ids`, canonical hashes of `native_candidate_refs`, and the identical `primary_family_basis_refs`. Its reopened `basis_refs` contain the exact native role-domain records and both primary AA records; `family_architecture_basis_statement` must explain family membership, the S obligation and cognate compatibility, with zero `unresolved_competing_type_or_architecture_interpretations`. These are attributed executed scientific reviews, never generated by this package or made true by hashes alone.

The producer and independently authored wrapper validate these conditions separately. Only after that stricter exceptional review does the independent wrapper pass a temporary generic TypeII subtype to the retained core, avoiding its obsolete IIG cardinality condition while retaining every source/domain/role/context check. The original native subtype and explicit reviewed subtype remain in the prepared queue/review provenance. An unsupported scalar `reviewed_subtype` change that the retained adapter would ignore is rejected. Missing S, partial/truncated S, ambiguous family or unsupported model-to-family mapping remains partial/uncertain; it cannot become0. Supported complete loci retain1 under the documented positive-presence semantics.

## Review, states and binary/count semantics

`apply` rebuilds the actual queue and requires exact equality with preparation. It consumes an executed review for every raw domain, native/rejected candidate, unmapped model, source context, empty sequence and circular-origin item, plus four type-specific whole-source/full-scope coverage reviews. Each decision reopens actual typed records. Manual candidates can use existing fullscope native domains/source neighborhoods; no new HMM search is performed. Unresolved reviewed interpretations are valid outcomes. Output remains `INDEPENDENT_CURATED_VALIDATION_REQUIRED`.

The JSON scientific cell vocabulary is `NOT_RUN`, `FAILED`, `UNCERTAIN`, `PARTIAL_CANDIDATE`, `CURATED_PARTIAL`, `COMPLETE_PREDICTED`, `NOT_DETECTED_AFTER_SUCCESSFUL_SEARCH`. Search completion and architecture presence remain separate. A failed corroboration run is still failed even when a reliable complete architecture is present.

`rm_matrix.py` exports exactly196 rows and784 long cells, initialized to NOT_RUN when a reviewed genome result is absent. It writes no inferred biological output by itself. Final values are:

- `1`: at least one unambiguously accepted complete predicted locus. Other partial/unresolved loci or incomplete search coverage do not erase established presence. A conflict invalidating the entire accepted positive basis sets `positive_invalidated=True` and suppresses1; it is never a flag for merely having another uncertain copy.
- `0`: `NOT_DETECTED_AFTER_SUCCESSFUL_SEARCH`, both detectors fully validated, every relevant source sequence/profile/domain/context accounted, no accepted or unresolved qualifying candidate and an executed fullscope coverage review. This is method-bounded non-detection, not proof of biological absence.
- `NA`: no accepted complete positive with partial, unreviewed, failed, not-run or unresolved evidence. Reviewed partial does not become a binary1.

`complete_count` and `partial_count` are accepted locus counts, not raw call-row counts; `candidate_count` counts non-excluded candidate outcomes. Counts are lower bounds when `count_lower_bound=True`. `conflict=True` means additional unresolved coverage/candidates or incomplete search, and does not erase a valid1. No fractional/zero-filled imputation occurs. A reviewed orphan MTase/generic nuclease/repair disposition remains in evidence and is excluded from a specific R-M type only with executed basis.
