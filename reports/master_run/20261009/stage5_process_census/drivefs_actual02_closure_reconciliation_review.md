## A bounded no-reboot alternative

A complete authoritative process-universe snapshot, disjoint from a proven closed birth interval containing every possible old worker, can establish a new current-quiescence proof. This is logically different from looking up one missing PID. It does not establish historical exit code0, an old empty-job query, or success of the failed DriveFS step.

The proof needs all of the following, supplied by a separately reviewed operator/checker:

1. Reopen and bind the exact original sources, request, STOP, result, owner identity, Linux/WSL receipts and original-lock identity. Establish that the old owner cannot perform another launch; an authenticated retained owner kernel terminal is the preferred witness. The result timestamp is emitted before owner exit and cannot itself be an owner-exit upper bound.
2. Prove a conservative inclusive worker birth interval. The recorded owner birth is its lower bound. The worker was created before the function raised and before the result was written, so an authenticated result-write/launcher witness can bound this single worker's creation separately from the owner exit. Enlarge bounds for conversion/rounding precision and any actual clock discontinuity; uncertain bounds veto the method. Do not treat CIM microsecond dates as exact 100ns FILETIME values.
3. Obtain a fresh complete kernel process snapshot with birth identity for every potentially live or terminating process, validate all lengths/offsets/counts and the actual API status, and preserve its bytes and interpretation. Unknown entries, inaccessible birth information, partial enumeration, truncation, unsupported layouts or unqualified provider omissions veto the proof. WMI CreationDate and a successful WMI query alone are not documented promises of atomic complete coverage. PID/name/parent filters must not shrink the universe before completeness is established.
4. Require no process birth interval intersecting the conservative old-worker interval. A candidate or unknown is a veto; do not terminate an unrelated process just because its birth falls in that interval. PID reuse does not erase an old process that could still run. Repeat or bracket observations to expose races, while retaining the authoritative snapshot/completeness argument; repetition alone cannot repair missing coverage.
5. Persist a separate CURRENT_QUIESCENCE_BY_COMPLETE_PROCESS_UNIVERSE_DISJOINTNESS receipt with all premises, qualifications and raw witnesses. Keep historical worker terminal and historical job-empty states UNRECOVERED. Any later exact STOP removal requires root-owned explicit scope authority, matching original STOP bytes, publication/readback and unchanged original guards. This review supplies no removal authority.

[Win32_Process](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/cimwin32prov/win32-process) documents creation dates and PID reuse; it does not by itself provide the stronger snapshot contract above. [NtQuerySystemInformation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/winternl/nf-winternl-ntquerysysteminformation) documents the native system-information API, whose actual class/layout and complete birth fields would need separate source/fixture validation before use.
